# Intersection Theory ↔ Eisenbud–Harris

Companion repository: https://github.com/walnut-burgundy/Eisenbud-Harris

Fulton's *Intersection Theory* and the Eisenbud–Harris sequence should be read as complementary rather than redundant.

- Eisenbud's *Commutative Algebra: with a View Toward Algebraic Geometry* supplies algebraic mechanisms: localization, associated primes, dimension/codimension, flatness, differentials, homological methods, free resolutions, and Gröbner methods.
- Eisenbud–Harris, *The Geometry of Schemes*, turns that algebra into scheme-theoretic geometry.
- Eisenbud–Harris, *3264 and All That*, develops concrete intersection-theory technique through Chow rings, Grassmannians, Chern classes, projective bundles, Segre classes, degeneracy loci, excess intersection, blow-ups, and GRR.
- Fulton supplies the systematic intersection-theory framework and reference.
