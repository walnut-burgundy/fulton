## What changed in the second edition

Sagan's second-edition preface identifies two large changes without requiring us to synthesize them yet:

1. the treatment of the hook formula was changed to use the Novelli–Pak–Stoyanovskii bijection;
2. a new applications chapter was added, covering differential posets, growths/local rules, group actions on posets and unimodality, and chromatic symmetric functions.
